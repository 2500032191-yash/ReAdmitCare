Advanced Boosting

The original dataset is highly imbalanced because the number of patients who were not readmitted within 30 days is much larger than the number of patients who were readmitted.

Improved XGBoost

An improved XGBoost model was trained using scale_pos_weight to give more importance to the minority class.

Precision

18.22%

Recall

60.50%

F1-Score

28.01%

Why Improvement Was Needed

The original XGBoost model achieved high accuracy but had very low recall for the minority class.

The improved model focuses more strongly on identifying patients who may be readmitted within 30 days.

Important:

The model is an academic machine-learning demonstration. It should not be used as a medical diagnosis or clinical decision-making system.
