Classification Models

The classification task predicts whether a patient is at risk of 30-day hospital readmission.

The dataset is highly imbalanced, so recall and F1-score are especially important evaluation metrics.

Important:

Because the dataset contains many more non-readmitted patients than readmitted patients, accuracy alone can be misleading.

For this reason, ReAdmitCare considers recall, precision, F1-score and ROC-AUC when evaluating classification models.
